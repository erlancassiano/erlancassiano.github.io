Case study

VCUB1 final flight assembly

Final flight assembly of the 12 kg VCUB1 CubeSat, including delta vibration and solar-panel deployment checks ahead of Falcon 9 launch.

Visiona · 12 kg CubeSat · Falcon 9 Transporter-7 · 2023

  • CAD
  • Test
  • Additive Manufacturing

Context

VCUB1 is a 12 kg CubeSat that was launched on Falcon 9 (SpaceX Transporter-7, April 2023) and operated in orbit. In early 2023 I supported final integration and performed the final flight assembly.

Requirements

  • Complete flight assembly with controlled procedures for solar panels and deployer interfaces.
  • Delta vibration and solar-panel deployment verification before launch.
  • 6U deployer-rail fit-check and integration tooling readiness for Falcon 9.
