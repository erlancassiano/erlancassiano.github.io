Case study

SATVHR mechanical architecture & STM vibration qualification

Mechanical and thermal architecture of a below-300 kg LEO Earth-observation satellite, including STM vibration qualification at INPE/LIT (May 2026).

Visiona · LEO Earth observation · Phase B/C · 2023present

Context

SATVHR is a below-300 kg, 5-year-life LEO Earth-observation satellite in phase B/C. As Mechanical Architect, I own the mechanical and thermal architecture: Creo layouts, payload accommodation, sandwich-panel structure, mass budget, and interface control documents (ICDs).

Requirements

  • Mechanical configuration that accommodates the payload within a below-300 kg system mass budget.
  • Sandwich-panel primary structure sized for launch environments (flight: CFRP faces; STM: aluminium faces on aluminium honeycomb).
  • GD&T and tolerance stack-up per ISO GPS on mechanical drawings.
  • Structuralthermal model (STM) vibration qualification correlated with the FEM.

Approach

Layouts and accommodation were driven in Creo. Structural work used Femap/Nastran models for design reviews and troubleshooting; thermal architecture used Thermal Desktop/SINDA. Margin reports documented the structural and thermal posture ahead of system reviews (PRR, PDR), with system / structure / thermal CDRs targeted for end of 2026.

Results

< 300 kgSystem mass class
May 2026STM vib. qual. passed
< 0.1 µmJitter measurement precision

STM vibration qualification (sine, random, and sine burst) passed at INPE/LIT in May 2026 and was correlated with the FEM. Jitter on the STM (main telescope mirror displacement under reaction-wheel imbalance) was measured with precision better than 0.1 µm.
