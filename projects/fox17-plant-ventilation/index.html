Case study

Compressor-room ventilation & plant piping (cement plant)

Cement-plant mechanical engineering: compressor-room ventilation with CFD, compressed-air and industrial-water piping, and a palletizer reuse study (2016–17).

Fox17 · cement plant · 2016–17

  • CAD
  • CFD
  • Piping

Context

At Fox17 I supported cement-plant mechanical design covering compressor-room ventilation, compressed-air and industrial-water piping, and reuse studies for palletizer filtration.

Requirements

  • Ventilation concept for a compressor room, verified with CFD.
  • Compressed-air and industrial-water piping layouts.
  • Palletizer filter reuse study.

Approach

CAD layouts for ducts and piping; CFD velocity checks in SolidWorks Flow Simulation; site coordination against as-built ducts.

Results

Ventilation and piping packages delivered for plant execution; CFD used to check duct velocity along the network.

Manufacturing & test

Field photos document duct installation and the compressor room as built.

Lessons

Pairing CFD spot-checks with CAD piping layouts catches velocity bottlenecks before fabrication.

Tools

  • SolidWorks
  • SolidWorks Flow Simulation
  • AutoCAD

Confidentiality

Client names on this case study are kept generic. No confidential Visiona or Thales Alenia Space data are published here. Title-block text, prices, and program chrome were cropped or redacted from CAD screenshots where present.
