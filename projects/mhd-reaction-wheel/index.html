MHD magnetic-fluid reaction wheel

M.Sc. thesis at ITA: development of a magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) magnetic-fluid reaction wheel for CubeSat attitude control.

ITA M.Sc. thesis · in progress · 2025–2027

  • CAD
  • Calculations
  • Test
  • Additive Manufacturing

Context

Master’s research at Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA), programme EAM-1 — Aeronautical Design, Structures, and Aerospace Systems. Advisor: Prof. Tiago de Paula. Expected completion July 2027.

Requirements

  • Reaction-wheel function using a magnetic fluid under MHD actuation, sized for CubeSat attitude control.

Approach

Thesis work in progress on modelling and prototype development for a magnetic-fluid reaction wheel. Public technical detail will be added as results are cleared for release.

Results

Research in progress (expected completion July 2027). Measured performance figures are not published here yet.
