Case study

Lifting & handling MGSE — concept

Led the concept for the lifting and handling MGSE; detailed design and build by a subcontracted supplier; tested during the STM AIT campaign.

Visiona · SATVHR STM AIT · concept lead

  • CAD
  • Test
  • Calculations

Context

Ground support equipment was required to lift and handle the SATVHR STM safely during AIT and vibration preparation at INPE/LIT.

Requirements

  • Safe lift / handle interfaces compatible with the STM mechanical architecture.
  • Supplier-deliverable detailed design and manufacture from a clear concept package.
  • Verification during the STM AIT campaign.

Approach

I led the concept; detailed design and manufacturing were executed by a subcontracted supplier. Concept work covered lift points, handling envelopes, and interface definition against the STM configuration.

Results

MGSE was manufactured by the supplier and tested during the STM AIT campaign.

Manufacturing & test

Detailed and built by a subcontractor; functional/handling checks during STM AIT. TVAC for the satellite: not yet done (separate from MGSE).

Tools

  • Creo Parametric
  • ICD definition
  • Supplier follow-up

Confidentiality

No confidential Visiona or Thales Alenia Space data, drawings, or datasets are published on this page. TVAC for SATVHR is not yet done and is not claimed here.
