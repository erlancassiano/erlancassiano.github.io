Case study

Steel chimney, ~95 m with helical strakes (cement plant)

~95.5 m cement-plant steel chimney with helical strakes — three bolted structural flanges, baseplate at Ø5.04 m — checked for vortex shedding, wind, seismic and fatigue in MECASTACK. Technical data report and QC/approval.

Fox17 · cement plant · 2015

  • CAD
  • Calculations
  • FEA

Context

In 2015 at Fox17 I wrote the technical data report and performed QC/approval for a ~95.5 m steel chimney (top elevation 115 m context) with helical strakes for a cement plant.

Requirements

  • ~95.5 m stack with helical strakes; base diameter 5.04 m.
  • Three bolted structural flanges plus baseplate.
  • Checks for vortex shedding, wind, seismic and fatigue.

Approach

Structural modelling and verification in MECASTACK; 3D detailing of flanges and baseplate for fabrication.

Results

~95.5 mStack height
Ø5.04 mBase diameter
3 + baseBolted flanges
MECASTACKWind / seismic / fatigue

Helical strakes included for vortex-shedding control; report authored with QC/approval responsibility.

Manufacturing & test

Flange and baseplate models prepared for fabrication; stack segmented at bolted joints.

Lessons

Combining aerodynamic appendages (strakes) with bolted segmentation is a useful pattern for tall slender structures.

Tools

  • MECASTACK
  • SolidWorks
  • Technical reporting
