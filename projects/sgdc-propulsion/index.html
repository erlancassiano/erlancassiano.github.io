Case study

SGDC propulsion technology transfer

On-the-job training on Brazil’s SGDC bipropellant propulsion subsystem; showed that more than 16 kg of additional propellant could be loaded, giving ~2.5 months of life and ~R$ 36M.

Thales Alenia Space · Cannes · AEB programme · 2014–2016

  • Calculations
  • Test
  • CAD

Context

Selected by the Brazilian Space Agency (AEB) for the SGDC Technology Absorption Programme at Thales Alenia Space in Cannes: about 5,500 h of full-time on-the-job training on the SGDC bipropellant propulsion subsystem (tanks, valves, lines, interfaces), from requirements and design through production, procedures, testing and operation.

Requirements

  • Understand and follow the bipropellant propulsion lifecycle under ECSS-driven V&V as a trainee on SGDC.
  • Assess propulsion performance over life (pressure, thrusters, temperature, propellant gauging), including tank deformation under pressurisation where relevant to loading.

Approach

Worked across the bipropellant propulsion lifecycle with Thales engineers. Showed that more than 16 kg of additional propellant could be loaded on SGDC when accounting for tank deformation under pressurisation.

Results

> 16 kgAdditional propellant
~2.5 monthsExtra service life
~R$ 36MLife-extension value shown

AEB later recognised the propellant-loading method that extends SGDC lifetime (Mar 2017). Witnessed SGDC propulsion-related tests in Cannes as a trainee.
