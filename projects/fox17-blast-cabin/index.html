Case study

Blast-cleaning cabin in a 40-ft container

Blast-cleaning cabin integrated into a 40-ft shipping container — fabrication photos from the 2015 build.

Fox17 · fabrication · 2015

  • CAD
  • Manufacturing

Context

Fox17 project to fit a blast-cleaning cabin inside a standard 40-ft container for portable abrasive cleaning.

Requirements

  • Fit blast-cabin structure and workflow inside a 40-ft ISO container envelope.

Approach

Structural fit-out of the container interior for blast operations.

Results

Cabin fabricated inside the container shell for field deployment.

Manufacturing & test

Fabrication documented in shop photos during assembly.

Lessons

Containerized process equipment forces tight packaging and access planning.

Tools

  • SolidWorks
  • Fabrication
