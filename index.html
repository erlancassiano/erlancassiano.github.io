Work Experience

  1. 2023 – present

    Mechanical Architect — Spacecraft Mechanical Design

    Visiona Tecnologia Espacial · São José dos Campos

    • Mechanical and thermal architecture of SATVHR, a below-300 kg, 5-year-life LEO satellite (phase B/C): Creo layouts, payload accommodation, mass budget, ICDs.
    • GD&T and ISO GPS tolerance stack-up on mechanical drawings; sandwich panels (Al honeycomb; CFRP faces on flight, Al faces on STM).
    • Led the lifting/handling MGSE concept (detailed and built by a subcontractor); co-wrote the platform thermal-controller specification through acceptance.
    • Direct and review structural/thermal analyses (Femap/Nastran, Thermal Desktop/SINDA); author margin reports; present mechanical/thermal at PRR and PDR toward CDRs (end 2026).
    • Co-authored the STM test plan/specification; led STM vibration qualification at INPE/LIT (passed May 2026), correlated with the FEM; measured STM jitter with precision better than 0.1 µm.
