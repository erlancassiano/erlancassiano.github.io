Experience

Timeline

From propulsion technology transfer on Brazil’s SGDC to mechanical architecture on SATVHR.

  1. 2023 – present

    Space Systems Engineer — Mechanical Architect

    Visiona Tecnologia Espacial · São José dos Campos

    • Mechanical and thermal architecture of SATVHR, a below-300 kg, 5-year-life LEO satellite (phase B/C): Creo layouts, payload accommodation, mass budget, ICDs.
    • GD&T and ISO GPS tolerance stack-up on mechanical drawings; sandwich panels (Al honeycomb; CFRP faces on flight, Al faces on STM).
    • Led the lifting/handling MGSE concept (detailed and built by a subcontractor); co-wrote the platform thermal-controller specification through acceptance.
    • Direct and review structural/thermal analyses (Femap/Nastran, Thermal Desktop/SINDA); author margin reports; present mechanical/thermal at PRR and PDR toward CDRs (end 2026).
    • Co-authored the STM test plan/specification; led STM vibration qualification at INPE/LIT (passed May 2026), correlated with the FEM; measured STM jitter with precision better than 0.1 µm.

  2. Jan – Mar 2023

    Space Systems Engineer — VCUB1 CubeSat

    Visiona Tecnologia Espacial

    • Final flight assembly of the 12 kg VCUB1 CubeSat; delta vibration and solar-panel deployment tests; deployer-rail fit-check and Falcon 9 (Transporter-7) integration tooling.
    • Co-author, IAC 2024 paper on the VCUB1 thermal-vacuum test campaign.

  3. 2016 – 2017

    SET-G Fellow — Satellite Engineering (SINDAE)

    CNPq / AEB / INPE · Brasília

    One-year fellowship supporting educational devices and modelling missions, engines and launchers for investment planning.

  4. 2014 – 2016

    Satellite Propulsion Engineer — SGDC technology transfer

    Thales Alenia Space · Cannes, France

    • Selected by AEB for ~5,500 h on-the-job training on the SGDC bipropellant propulsion subsystem.
    • Showed that more than 16 kg of additional propellant could be loaded (tank deformation under pressurisation), giving ~2.5 months of life and ~R$ 36M.

    • Funcate: junior space project analyst — AEB model-rocketry docs; Alcântara authorisations.
    • Autotrac: SolidWorks test-rig structures; CNC prototypes.
