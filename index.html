About

Mechanical engineer with experience since 2014: Mechanical Architect and Space Systems Engineer at Visiona Tecnologia Espacial (since 2023) and propulsion trainee at Thales Alenia Space (2014–2016). I cover the lifecycle from requirements to qualification on geostationary (Brazil's SGDC) and low Earth orbit Earth-observation (SATVHR) missions, and lead SATVHR's mechanical and thermal architecture, mass budget and interfaces.

Highlights

  • SATVHR – lead the mechanical and thermal architecture of a below-300 kg LEO Earth-observation satellite (5-year life, phase B/C).
  • STM vibration qualification passed at INPE/LIT in May 2026 (sine, random and sine burst), correlated with the FEM.
  • SGDC – showed that more than 16 kg of additional propellant could be loaded, adding ~2.5 months of service life (~R$ 36M).
  • VCUB1 – final flight assembly of the 12 kg CubeSat, launched on Falcon 9 (Transporter-7, 2023) and operated in orbit.
  • Team – manage 4 junior engineers and 2 interns since 2023.
  • Jitter – measured jitter on the STM with precision better than 0.1 µm.
